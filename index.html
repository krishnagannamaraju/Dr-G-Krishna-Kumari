About

Senior academic with 25+ years in teaching, curriculum design, academic leadership and research supervision. Her doctoral work in applied mathematics modelled how biological fluids move, and she now applies AI and machine learning to problems in agriculture, language and security. She is a research supervisor at JNTU Hyderabad and is completing a second Ph.D. in CSE (Artificial Intelligence) at S R University, Warangal.

Research

Fluid mechanics and mathematical modelling

Peristaltic transport of Jeffrey, Casson, Carreau, micropolar and power-law fluids through porous channels and tubes; nanofluids; magnetohydrodynamics; heat transfer. Includes a UGC-funded minor research project (₹1,95,000) on pumping of nanofluids with different flow geometries.

AI for agriculture

Image segmentation for weed detection and removal, and federated spatio-temporal models for crop disease detection and precision agriculture.

Machine learning and language

Sentiment analysis of hotel reviews with BERT, VADER and XLNet; crime prediction with IGWO-SVM; big data and IoT applications.

Operations research

Combined transportation and inventory optimisation models with multiple time periods and multiple objectives.
